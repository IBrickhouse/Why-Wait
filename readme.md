# In my many years of experiencing theme parks, I often found myself wondering about how theme parks operate. As a masters student in analytics at NTU, I decided to use my skills and begin tracking data to answer lifelong  questions about the experience. Beginning with analyzing my newly local theme park, Universal Studios Singapore. The first question I am hoping to answer is "What factors are associated with longer wait times?". Once I have that question answered, I hope to answer "By how much does each factor change the wait time?"

## The basic qualities of the attraction / show that I will be tracking:<br/>
  * Attraction / Show Name<br/>
  * Date<br/>
  * Time<br/>
  * Wait Time<br/>
  * Attractions status<br/>

## Theoretical aspects I think that could impact wait times and may or may not be able to find data for:<br/>
  * Weather Condition<br/>
  * Total Park Attendance that day<br/>
  * Most recent media associated with attraction/show<br/>
  * Historical impact / popularity of property<br/>
